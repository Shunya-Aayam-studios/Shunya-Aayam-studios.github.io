SHUNYA AAYAM STUDIOS - PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 5, 2026
Shunya Aayam Studios ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data and proprietary business data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (https://shunya-aayam-studios.github.io/) or use our AI modeling services.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Personal & Business Information: When you contact us, request a quote, or sign up for our services, we may collect your name, business name, email address (e.g., via shunyaaayam.ai@gmail.com), phone number, and billing information.

1.2 Input Data (Images & Garments): To provide our AI modeling services, we collect the reference images, flat-lays, and design files you upload or email to us.

1.3 Automatically Collected Data: When you visit our website, we may collect standard log data, IP addresses, browser types, and usage metrics via cookies and analytics tools to improve our website's performance.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • Service Delivery: To generate the AI photoshoots and custom avatars you requested.
  • Communication: To send you deliverables, invoices, updates, and marketing materials (you may opt-out of marketing at any time).
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access to our systems.

3. HOW WE HANDLE YOUR IMAGES & AI TRAINING

Confidentiality of Client Garments: We understand that your apparel designs are your intellectual property. The garment reference images you provide are used strictly for the purpose of generating your deliverables.
Base Model Training: Shunya Aayam Studios does not use your proprietary garment designs or non-public reference images to train public, open-source AI foundational models without your explicit, written consent.

4. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. We may share information only with:

  • Trusted Service Providers: Third-party payment processors, cloud hosting services (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud), or secure AI compute nodes necessary to fulfill our services, all of whom are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights and safety of Shunya Aayam Studios.

5. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

We implement industry-standard organizational and technical measures to protect your data. Final outputs and input references are securely stored in our cloud architecture for a limited period to facilitate revisions, after which they may be archived or deleted according to our data retention schedule.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at: shunyaaayam.ai@gmail.com.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal or regulatory requirements regarding AI and data privacy. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date.
